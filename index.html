How to use a product link

  1. Open the CAFANS listing and check the size chart, color options and current price.
  2. If the original seller URL is available, paste it into HooBuy and confirm that the item loads and can be ordered.
  3. Check the destination shipping estimate. If warehouse QC photos are available after purchase, review the size tag, color and visible condition before authorizing shipment.

HooBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a HooBuy spreadsheet?

A HooBuy spreadsheet organizes shopping finds, listing links and reference prices. Use this independent CAFANS catalog to compare products and locate the listing you want to check with your shopping agent.

Can I order every listed item through HooBuy?

Availability depends on the original seller and HooBuy's current rules. Confirm that HooBuy accepts the seller URL and that the size and color you want are available.

How should I compare listing photos and QC photos?

Use listing photos to identify the intended style. When warehouse QC photos are provided, compare the received item's size tag, color and condition with the options you ordered.
